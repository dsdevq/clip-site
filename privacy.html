Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This policy covers the Google app named "clip", a personal tool used only by the owner.

Google data the app accesses

The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels, through the permissions the owner grants: YouTube upload, YouTube channel management, and YouTube Analytics read-only access.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos and to read their statistics. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

The access tokens are stored on the owner's own server, on an encrypted disk. They are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.

Other users

The app is not offered to other users. No other person's data is collected or processed.

Retention and revocation

No data is retained beyond the access tokens. The owner can revoke them at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

YouTube API Services

clip uses YouTube API Services. Use of those services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/dsdevq/clip-site/issues.
