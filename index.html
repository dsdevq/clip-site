clip

clip is the owner's own command-line tool. It cuts, narrates and captions short vertical videos and uploads them to YouTube channels the owner controls.

The Google app named "clip" is used only by the owner, to upload to and manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users, and no one else can sign in to it.

Read the privacy policy for details on which Google data the app accesses and how it is handled.
